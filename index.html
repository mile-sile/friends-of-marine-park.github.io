Who We Are

A community rooted in the sea

Formed in 2017, Friends of Marine Park (FMP) is a passionate, ground-led community network supported by Singapore's National Parks Board (NParks) under the "Friends of the Parks" initiative.

FMP acts as a bridge between the government and the community, bringing together representatives from over 40 marine-related organisations — divers, anglers, boaters, educators, marine researchers, and dedicated volunteers.

"Healthy Seas, Connected Communities, and a Resilient Future."

We believe in fostering trust among different sea users, balancing recreation with conservation, and cultivating a strong "conservation culture" to protect Singapore's marine biodiversity.

Where We Work

Our waters, our responsibility

Our primary conservation and community efforts are centred around Singapore's Southern Islands.

01

Sisters' Islands Marine Park

Singapore's First & Only Marine Park · 40 Hectares

A sanctuary for marine life, featuring a 220m floating boardwalk, lagoon tidal pools, and underwater dive trails. We support outreach and education here, helping visitors safely interact with the park's rich ecosystems.

02

Bendera Bay

St John's Island · 3.9 Hectares

A sheltered lagoon featuring a rich mix of mangroves, corals, sandy shores, and seagrass. Bendera Bay is FMP's dedicated shared space where researchers, boaters, and educators collaborate safely.

What We Do

Our Initiatives

We champion initiatives that allow the public to enjoy our waters responsibly while actively preserving marine ecosystems.

01 · Conservation

Conservation & Research

Hawksbill Turtle Headstarting

We support NParks and the Singapore Oceanarium to protect the critically endangered Hawksbill sea turtle, rearing hatchlings until they are strong enough to survive in the wild.

Citizen Science Dives

Volunteer research divers conduct regular biodiversity surveys and coral monitoring at Bendera Bay and the Sisters' Islands to track the health of local ecosystems.

02 · Education

Education & Outreach

Intertidal Walks

FMP volunteers guide the public through intertidal zones during low tide, educating them on how to interact gently with marine life such as Haddon's carpet anemones, flower crabs, and sea stars.

Heritage Appreciation

At Bendera Bay, we collaborate with former islanders to display traditional kolek (wooden boats) and share the heritage of Singapore's coastal communities.

03 · Community

Community & Stewardship

Codes of Conduct

Working with kayakers, boaters, and guides to develop and implement strict, environmentally responsible Codes of Conduct for using marine spaces.

Hands-On Workshops

Regular sustainable fishing workshops, coastal beach clean-ups, and public outreach booths to raise awareness about the impact of pollution on coastal ecology.

Get Involved

The ocean needs you

Protecting Singapore's big blue backyard is a collective effort. We welcome individuals and organisations who have the sea at heart.

Volunteer

Join our regular beach clean-ups and help us weigh and remove marine debris from Bendera Bay. Every pair of hands makes a difference.

Participate

Sign up for our sustainable fishing workshops or citizen science dives. Experience the marine park first-hand while contributing to its protection.

Advocate

Practice "light-touch" eco-tourism and follow our marine Codes of Conduct whenever you visit the Southern Islands. Inspire others to do the same.
