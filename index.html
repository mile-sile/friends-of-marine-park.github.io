Protecting Singapore's Big Blue Backyard

Formed in 2017, we are a community network supported by NParks, bringing together divers, boaters, and volunteers to protect our marine biodiversity.

Volunteer With Us

Who We Are

Guided by our Chairman, Stephen Beng, the Friends of Marine Park (FMP) acts as a bridge between the government and over 40 marine-related organizations. We balance recreation with conservation to cultivate a strong conservation culture in Singapore.

Where We Work

Sisters' Islands Marine Park

Singapore's first marine park spanning 40 hectares. We support safe interaction with the lagoon tidal pools and underwater dive trails.

Bendera Bay (St John’s Island)

A 3.9-hectare sheltered lagoon and our dedicated shared space for researchers, educators, and the community to collaborate safely.

What We Do

  • Conservation: Hawksbill Turtle Headstarting and Citizen Science biodiversity dives.
  • Education: Guided intertidal walks and marine heritage appreciation.
  • Stewardship: Establishing Codes of Conduct and hosting sustainable fishing workshops.
